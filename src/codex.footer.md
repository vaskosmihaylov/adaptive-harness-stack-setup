## Sandbox and approvals

Configured defaults are `sandbox_mode = "workspace-write"` and `approval_policy = "on-request"`.
Ask for escalation when a command genuinely needs network or writes outside the workspace — say
what it needs and why, rather than working around the sandbox.

Reasoning effort is set in `~/.codex/config.toml`. Raise it for a hard debugging or migration
task; don't ask me to raise it for routine edits.
